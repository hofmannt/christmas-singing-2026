# Christmas singing 2026

Practice material for our quartet: two women, two men.

## Aura Lee

Barbershop arrangement (SPEBSQSA, 1973), set for a mixed quartet.
The women sing tenor and lead (the melody) an octave above the original men's version; the men sing baritone and bass as written.

**Score:** [Aura_Lee_mixed_quartet_score.pdf](aura-lee/Aura_Lee_mixed_quartet_score.pdf)

**Practice tracks** (♩ = 72; each track starts with a pitch pipe on A♭ and four clicks):

To listen: open a track and click **View raw** (or the download button) to play it in the browser or save it to your phone.

**Ranges**

- Tenor: B♭4 to B♭5 (the B♭ comes once, in bar 31)
- Lead: E♭4 to E♭5
- Baritone: E♭3 to D♭4
- Bass: A♭2 to A♭3, plus one low E♭2 in bar 31 (sing the higher E♭ shown in brackets if you need to)

`aura-lee/source/` holds the transcription and the scripts that build the score (LilyPond) and the audio (FluidSynth).
